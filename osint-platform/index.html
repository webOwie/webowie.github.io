OSINT · Research automation · Local AI

From open sources to controlled intelligence workflows.

webOwie is being developed as a modular environment for research-oriented search, source integration, enrichment, analysis and reproducible OSINT workflows. The platform combines investigation tooling with local-first AI and the infrastructure needed to operate it deliberately.

Core capabilities

  • Research-oriented search and source integration
  • Structured enrichment and analysis workflows
  • Local and hybrid AI assistance
  • Reproducible investigation processes
  • Source-aware documentation and evidence-oriented workflows
  • Operator-controlled infrastructure and automation

Different from a single-purpose OSINT tool

Traditional OSINT products concentrate on search, reconnaissance or link analysis. webOwie is designed around the surrounding system as well: model routing, automation, infrastructure, privacy controls, deployment and operational governance.

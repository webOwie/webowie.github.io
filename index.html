Working software · Infrastructure Client

Control the platform, not just another dashboard.

The webOwie Infrastructure Client is the operator-side control plane for people who actually build and run the infrastructure underneath webOwie. It connects to Proxmox VE and is being developed around infrastructure discovery, inventory, network fabric, security policy, DNS/ACME and safe deployment workflows.

Built for

  • Proxmox and virtualization operators
  • DevOps / platform engineering
  • Network and security administrators
  • Technical consultants and self-hosting power users
  • webOwie platform operators responsible for cluster, DNS, PKI, storage, backup and lifecycle
Not the office client

Business users do not need this cockpit

A user who only works with webOwie for research, projects, reports, communication or normal business tasks gains nothing from a Proxmox and network control plane. A separate webOwie Client is planned for that audience, with the infrastructure complexity deliberately kept out of the way.

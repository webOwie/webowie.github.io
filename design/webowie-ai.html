Rules

  • Keep the webOwie parent symbol unchanged.
  • Use cyan only as the semantic signal/accent colour in the canonical logo.
  • Keep the AI module visually subordinate to webOwie.
  • Use the simplified favicon below 48 px.
  • Do not use decorative gradients, glow, bevel or 3D effects in canonical assets.
  • Do not merge the AI letters into the parent symbol.
  • Use monochrome variants only where colour is unavailable.
  • Preserve clear space around the full lockup.

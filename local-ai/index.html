Local AI infrastructure you control.

webOwie engineers local and hybrid AI systems for organizations that want useful AI without surrendering infrastructure, data flows or operational control to a single cloud vendor.

What webOwie builds

  • Self-hosted LLM and inference environments
  • Hybrid local/API model routing
  • Private knowledge and RAG infrastructure
  • Agent and workflow automation with explicit safety gates
  • Proxmox-based compute and storage integration
  • Observability, access control and reproducible deployment

Why local-first

Local-first architecture can reduce unnecessary data exposure, improve operational independence and make infrastructure decisions auditable. External AI services can still be integrated where they provide a measurable advantage, but they are treated as replaceable components rather than the foundation of the system.

Designed for operators

webOwie combines AI deployment with the infrastructure underneath it: compute, virtualization, networking, DNS, certificates, storage, security policy and lifecycle automation. That avoids the familiar industry ritual of buying an AI layer and discovering later that nobody designed the system it has to run on.
